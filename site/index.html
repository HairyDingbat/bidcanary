Questions

Where do the tenders come from?

From Find a Tender, the UK government's official publication point for procurement notices under the Procurement Act 2023. Smaller contracts in Scotland and some regional portals aren't covered yet.

Can I trust the summaries?

They're written by AI from the notice itself and are a quick guide. Always read the full notice before you bid; every alert links straight to it.

How does the free trial work?

Confirm your email and you get Starter, every match every morning with summaries, free for 14 days. No card is needed, and nothing is charged. After 14 days you move to the free weekly email unless you choose to upgrade.

Do you have a referral scheme?

Yes. Every digest includes your own link. When a business you refer becomes a paying customer, you both get a month free.

How do I cancel?

Paid plans: cancel any time from the link in your receipt, and you'll drop back to the free plan. Free plan: one-click unsubscribe in every email.

Who is it for?

UK businesses of any size that sell to the public sector, in any sector: cleaning, security, facilities, construction and trades, IT, care, training, catering, consultancy and more. It's a business service and isn't sold to consumers.
