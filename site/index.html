Questions

Where do the tenders come from?

From Find a Tender, the UK government's official publication point for procurement notices under the Procurement Act 2023. Smaller contracts in Scotland and some regional portals aren't covered yet.

Can I trust the summaries?

They're written by AI from the notice itself and are a quick guide. Always read the full notice before you bid; every alert links straight to it.

How do I cancel?

Paid plans: cancel any time from the link in your receipt, and you'll drop back to the free plan. Free plan: one-click unsubscribe in every email.

Who is it for?

UK businesses in cleaning, security, grounds maintenance and facilities management. It's a business service and isn't sold to consumers.
