Accessibility statement

Last updated: [DATE]

We want everyone to be able to use Bid Canary. This website aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What we've done

  • Pages work with a keyboard, and there's a "skip to content" link.
  • Form fields have labels, and status messages are announced to screen readers.
  • Text and background colours are chosen for strong contrast.
  • Pages work on mobile and can be zoomed to 200% without losing content.
  • Animations are reduced if your device asks for reduced motion.
  • Our emails use simple layouts and real text, not images of text.

Known limitations

We have not yet had an independent accessibility audit. Tender details come from public notices written by the buyer, and we can't control how accessible those documents are.

Tell us about a problem

If something doesn't work for you, or you need information in a different format, email support@bidcanary.co.uk. We'll reply within 5 working days.
